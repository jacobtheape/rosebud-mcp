❀ Rosebud MCP — API Documentation

US flower delivery as Model Context Protocol tools, for the Muse Platform connector program.

The customer only ever talks to the agent. The agent uses these tools; Rosebud's backend runs the florist checkout — a headless browser at a national florist in live mode, fully simulated in the default fake mode. No customer ever touches a florist website.

Tools

ToolDescription
search_arrangementsBouquets for an occasion within the customer's total budget (flowers + delivery + tax + the $2.99 Rosebud fee).
get_quoteLocks the exact total with line items. Quote expires after 30 minutes and is single-use.
purchaseRuns the florist checkout. Refuses unless user_confirmed=true and the quote is valid, unexpired, and unused. Refuses to charge if the live total differs from the approved quote.
order_statusOrder details and status.
cancel_orderCancels an order. Requires user_confirmed=true.
delivery_confirmationDelivery proof details when the florist provides them.

search_arrangements

occasion required — birthday, anniversary, sympathy, get_well, congratulations, love
budget_max required — the most the customer will spend total, including delivery, tax, and the $2.99 fee
delivery_zip required — US ZIP (validated; rejects malformed and impossible ZIPs)
delivery_date required — YYYY-MM-DD

Returns options with arrangement_id, name, florist, product price, and description. Every returned option's estimated all-in cost is at or under budget_max — the budget is enforced against the total, not the flower price alone.

get_quote

arrangement_id, occasion required
first_name, last_name, address1, city, state, zip required — recipient
delivery_date, card_message, sender_name required
phone, special_instructions — optional (required by some florists)

Returns a quote_id plus line items — product, delivery, estimated tax, and the $2.99 Rosebud service fee as its own line item — and the exact total. The agent must present every line to the user and get explicit approval of that exact total before calling purchase.

purchase

quote_id required — unexpired, unused quote from get_quote
user_confirmed required — must be true; the agent's attestation that the user approved this exact quote
platform_payment_ref required — the platform's proof that the customer paid Rosebud the exact approved total (a Stripe payment intent id in live mode). Single-use; reused refs are refused.
payment_amount required — what the platform collected; must equal the quote total exactly or the purchase is refused.

Rosebud is the merchant of record: the customer pays Rosebud, and Rosebud pays the florist the merchant total (product + delivery + tax, without the $2.99 fee) using its own business payment method. Returns the order id, charged_to_customer, paid_to_florist, and the fee. Any drift between the florist's live merchant total and the approved amount refuses the purchase and refunds the customer payment. No customer card data is accepted or handled.

order_status · cancel_order · delivery_confirmation

order_status(order_id) — order details and status
cancel_order(order_id, user_confirmed=true) — refuses without explicit user approval; a successful cancellation refunds the customer payment
delivery_confirmation(order_id) — delivery proof details when the florist provides them

Money flow (merchant of record)

1. Customer approves the exact quote total (flowers + delivery
   + tax + the $2.99 Rosebud service fee).

2. The platform charges the customer that exact total and credits
   Rosebud's Stripe account, returning a platform_payment_ref.

3. purchase() verifies the payment ref (captured, exact amount,
   single-use), then Rosebud pays the florist the merchant total
   (WITHOUT the $2.99 fee) with Rosebud's own business payment
   method. The $2.99 stays with Rosebud as margin.

4. If the florist's live total drifts from the approved merchant
   total, the checkout refuses BEFORE charging and the customer
   payment is refunded. cancel_order refunds the customer too.

Raw customer card numbers never touch Rosebud's servers.

Ordering flow

1. Collect: recipient first + last name, full US street address,
   city, state, ZIP, delivery date, total budget (incl. the fee),
   card note, occasion, sender name (+ phone / instructions
   when the florist requires them)

2. search_arrangements(occasion, budget_max, delivery_zip,
   delivery_date) → options guaranteed within the total budget

3. get_quote for the chosen option → exact total with every
   line item, including the $2.99 Rosebud service fee.
   Present it all to the user and get explicit approval.

4. Platform charges the customer the exact approved total into
   Rosebud's account → platform_payment_ref.
   purchase(quote_id, user_confirmed=true, platform_payment_ref,
   payment_amount) → order id. Refused without approval, on
   expired/used/unknown quotes, on unverified or reused payment refs,
   on amount mismatch, or if the florist's live total drifts
   (customer payment is refunded on drift).

5. Track with order_status / delivery_confirmation until delivered.
   cancel_order needs its own explicit user approval and refunds
   the customer payment.

Safeguards

  • Authentication: bearer API key on every /mcp call; /health is the only public route.
  • Approval-gated spend: purchase and cancel_order refuse unless user_confirmed=true. Approval covers one exact quote and can never be pre-granted, batched, or automated.
  • Exact-total quotes: 30-minute TTL, single-use; unknown or reused quotes are rejected. The charged total always equals the approved quote total.
  • Total-drift refusal: if the florist's live merchant total differs from the approved amount, the purchase is refused before any charge, and the customer payment is refunded.
  • No customer card data: purchase accepts a platform payment ref, not card numbers. Raw customer card data never touches Rosebud's servers, so there is no customer PAN to log, store, or leak.
  • Single-use payments: payment refs are verified (captured, exact amount) and can never be reused — a reused ref is refused to prevent double-spending.
  • Budget honesty: search filters on estimated all-in cost, so a $79.99 bouquet is never shown for an $80 total budget when delivery, tax, and the fee would exceed it.
  • Safe default: the server runs in fake checkout mode unless live mode is explicitly enabled — it cannot place a real order by accident.

Service fee

A flat $2.99 Rosebud service fee applies per delivery, always shown as its own line item on every quote and order — never buried in the flower price. Because Rosebud is the merchant of record, the fee is simply Rosebud's margin: the customer pays Rosebud the all-in total, Rosebud pays the florist the merchant total, and the $2.99 stays with Rosebud. No separate fee collection is needed.

Checkout modes

ROSEBUD_CHECKOUT_MODE (default: fake)

  • fake — simulated national florist. No network, no real orders. Used for development, demos, and smoke tests. The server can never place a real order in this mode.
  • live — headless-browser checkout at a real national florist (ROSEBUD_FLORIST=teleflora or 1800flowers; requires Playwright + Chromium).
Status note: live-mode adapters are structural scaffolding — selectors are marked for validation against the live sites, and both florists run anti-bot measures (expect CAPTCHAs or blocks). No live purchase has been tested end to end. Do not enable live mode until selectors are verified and a real test purchase + cancellation succeed.

Example: initialize + call a tool

curl https://<your-host>/mcp \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ROSEBUD_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Accept: application/json, text/event-stream" \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"initialize",
       "params":{"protocolVersion":"2025-06-18",
       "capabilities":{},"clientInfo":{"name":"example","version":"1.0"}}}'

Then notifications/initialized, tools/list, and tools/call with {"name": "search_arrangements", "arguments": {...}}.

Source & deployment

Python (mcp SDK v2, Starlette, uvicorn). Ships with a Dockerfile and a render.yaml Blueprint for one-click hosting on Render; Railway and Fly.io can use the Dockerfile. Set ROSEBUD_API_KEY as an environment variable. Keep ROSEBUD_CHECKOUT_MODE at fake until live checkout is validated.
